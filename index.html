Common questions

My devices aren't showing up.
Make sure HomeBoard+ has permission to access your Home data: Settings > Privacy & Security > HomeKit > HomeBoard+.

Where is my data stored?
On your device and in your own iCloud/Home account. See the Privacy Policy.
