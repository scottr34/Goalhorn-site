HomeBoard+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

HomeBoard+ ("the app") is a smart-home dashboard for iPad. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

Information the app accesses

With your permission, the app reads and controls the accessories and scenes in your Apple Home (HomeKit) setup so it can display and operate them on your dashboard. This information is accessed on your device and is never sent to me or to any third party.

Information I collect

None. HomeBoard+ does not collect, store on any server, or transmit personal information. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Data sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Data storage

Any app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device and may sync through your own iCloud account if you have iCloud enabled. I do not have access to that data.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your control

You can revoke the app's access to your Home data at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > HomeKit. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
